# Lodge des Almadies (English)

> 3-star hotel with 36 rooms and suites in Dakar (Senegal), in the Almadies district, about 15 minutes from the city centre. L'ENVERS restaurant, swimming pool, a 70 m² meeting room (40 people), two boutiques. 24-hour front desk, free Wi-Fi, guarded parking, backup generator.

Address: Route de l’OCI, Almadies, Dakar, Senegal
Phone: (+221) 33 869 03 45
Email: hotel@lodgedesalmadies.com
Payment: FCFA, euro, US dollar, Visa and Mastercard cards
